# Site functionality and information architecture audit (2026-10-11)

Dated record of the read-only audit UX-IA-002, "Audyt funkcjonalności i architektury informacji wszystkich podstron", requested by the user on 2026-10-11. It covers every route in PL/EN/DE. The home page is covered by the stage 1.4 review; its findings stay in the backlog (STATUS Known issues 12–15) and are not repeated here.

- **Live status** of every finding (UXA-01 … UXA-18): [`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md), "UX audit tracker".
- **Client questions** (C-xx) and gap audit findings (GAP-xx) are only referenced here; their wording lives in [`client-confirmations.md`](client-confirmations.md) and [`gap-audit-2026-10-10.md`](gap-audit-2026-10-10.md).

## Scope and method

- **Priorities requested by the user:**
  1. functionality;
  2. navigation and findability;
  3. section order and information architecture;
  4. readability and mobile comfort;
  5. consistency.

  Design and photo replacement are not priorities. Assets are listed separately at the end, and none was replaced.
- **Read-only:** no code, content, asset or configuration was changed. This file, `STATUS.md`, `CHANGELOG_AI.md` and the documentation table in `AGENTS.md` record the result.
- **Environment:** local production build (the build of UX-IA-001 stage 1.3, unchanged since) on a temporary server on port 5055. Edge through Playwright, with reduced motion unless motion was the subject. The dev server on port 5000 was not running.
- **Forms:** every request to `/api/*` was intercepted in the browser, so nothing reached the server or any mailbox. Empty and incomplete submissions only; the fake values were `audit@example.invalid`, etc.
- **Checks:**
  - **Crawl:** all 42 pages (14 routes × PL/EN/DE). Recorded per page: status, title, H1, heading outline, navigation state, language links, links, documents, forms, the last section's links and console errors.
  - **Links:** every internal link and `#anchor` found in `<main>`, the header and the footer, plus each external link.
  - **Readability:** all pages at 390, 768 and 1440 px (overflow, text sizes, line length, heading levels, tables, small targets).
  - **Interactions:** enquiry dialog and page, careers dialog, contact router and directory, certificate viewer, language switch, mobile menu, 404 and legacy URLs, form result states.
  - **Phones (390 px):** where key elements sit on long pages; close-ups at device pixel ratio 2.
  - **Six user tasks** walked through in the browser.
  - **Full-page screenshots:** PL at 390/768/1440 px, DE at 390 px.
- **Not covered:**
  - Safari/Firefox, real phones and screen readers (STATUS Known issues 8);
  - the form delivery path (`test:forms` is not run against a mailbox);
  - the legal content of the privacy policy (GAP-16 … GAP-18).

## Summary

- **Routing and links are sound.**
  - All 42 pages answer 200 with one H1 and no console errors.
  - 274 internal links (193 paths, 96 anchors) resolve. The only flagged anchor, `#top` (the back-to-top link without JavaScript), needs no id: browsers scroll to the top for `#top`, and `test:totop` covers it.
  - 63 documents are served as PDF; the 6 external links answer; legacy URLs redirect (308 → 200).
- **One P0:** every unknown EN/DE URL turns into a request storm of about 500 requests per second from one open tab (UXA-01).
- **Three P1:**
  - a false failure message after switching language on the form result page (UXA-02);
  - a dead end for the slaughter plants' certificates (UXA-03);
  - contradictory routes for offal and UPPZ enquiries (UXA-04).
- **The information architecture works for the main tasks.** Buyer, supplier, candidate and company-data tasks take 1–3 clicks. The friction is in missing next steps at page ends, the position of key blocks on long phone pages, certificates on phones, and orientation on pages outside the menu.
- **Readability:**
  - no horizontal overflow on any page at 390/768/1440 px; only the certificate archive table scrolls inside its own labelled region;
  - smallest text 13 px, used for labels, tags, table headers and the breadcrumb;
  - no paragraph wider than about 95 characters per line; no skipped heading levels.

## Findings

Priorities: **P0** broken or harmful, fix before anything else. **P1** blocks or misleads a core task. **P2** real friction or inconsistency. **P3** polish.

### UXA-01 · P0 · Request storm on EN/DE 404 pages

- **Where:** every unknown URL under `/en` or `/de` that renders the not-found page through the catch-all route `app/[locale]/[...rest]`. The not-found page itself is `app/[locale]/not-found.tsx`.
- **Evidence:** after load, one tab keeps prefetching the 404 page's section links (`/en/products`, `/en/quality`, `/en/certificates`, `/en/sales-export`, or the DE equivalents) without end. Each request returns 200, and the proxy rewrites it (`x-nextjs-rewritten-path: /en/produkty`). Measured over 5 s:

  | URL | Requests | Per second |
  |---|---|---|
  | `/en/not-there` | 2610 | ≈ 520 |
  | `/de/gibt-es-nicht` (390 px) | 2660 | ≈ 530 |
  | `/en/p/news` | 2597 | ≈ 520 |
  | `/de/p/aktuelles` | 2383 | ≈ 480 |
  | `/en/Contact` | 2702 | ≈ 540 |

  For comparison, normal pages make 2–4 prefetches per second: `/nie-ma` and `/nie-ma-takiej-strony` (PL 404s), `/en/products` (a normal EN page) and `/en/products/xyz` (an EN 404 below an existing route).
- **Consequence:**
  - A visitor's device and data plan are drained while the tab stays open.
  - The server renders dynamic pages (the nonce CSP makes content pages dynamic) about 500 times per second per tab, which risks availability and cost.
  - It is triggered by realistic paths: old EN/DE URLs of the previous site that are not in the redirect map, capitalisation (`/en/Contact`) and mistyped links.
  - The page itself looks normal, so nobody notices.
- **Proposal:**
  1. Find the root cause: why the prefetch of the rewritten EN/DE slugs never settles when it starts from the catch-all 404.
  2. As an immediate, low-risk mitigation, use `prefetch={false}` on the 404 page's links.
  3. Add a browser regression check that counts requests for 5 s on an EN, a DE and a PL 404. The browser suites do not visit 404 pages (STATUS Known issues 10).

### UXA-02 · P1 · Language switch drops the query (result page, contact topic)

- **Where:** the header language switch builds `/en/<slug>` without the query string.
- **Evidence:**
  - On `/wyslano?form=enquiry&status=ok` ("Dziękujemy. Wiadomość została wysłana.") the EN link is `/en/sent`. That page says "The form could not be sent.", because the result page treats missing parameters as a failure (GAP-14).
  - On `/kontakt?temat=sell-cattle#pomoc` the links are `/en/contact` and `/de/kontakt`, so the chosen topic is lost.
- **Consequence:** a foreign customer who has just sent an enquiry and switches language is told it failed. They may send it again or call. On Kontakt they have to find their topic again.
- **Proposal:**
  - Keep the query string (and hash) in the language links of `sent` and `contact`; the parameters are language-neutral.
  - Show a neutral page when `/wyslano` has no parameters (GAP-14).
- **Test:** switch language after `status=ok` and the thank-you text stays; switch on a Kontakt topic and the same topic is open.

### UXA-03 · P1 · Certificates: dead end for the slaughter plants

- **Where:** Certyfikaty, "Pozostałe zakłady". It says "Aktualny certyfikat tego zakładu nie jest opublikowany na stronie." for Zakład uboju, Linne (SH) and Zakład uboju II, Wydrzyn (SH II), with no link. The same sentence is on the SH and SH II cards on Zakłady. On Produkty, the cards of products from Linne (Ćwierci, Podroby) have no certificate row at all.
- **Consequence:** a quality or purchasing employee who needs the certificate of the plant that supplies quarters and offal stops here. The route to the right person exists, `/kontakt?temat=quality#pomoc`, which lists the quality contacts of Linne, Wydrzyn and Lisków, but it is not offered.
- **Proposal:** next to each such sentence, a link "Zapytaj dział jakości tego zakładu" to `/kontakt?temat=quality#pomoc` (existing label of the contact router). On Produkty, the same sentence and link for products from Linne. No new facts.
- **Test:** the link opens the quality topic of the contact router.

### UXA-04 · P1 · Offal and UPPZ: contradictory routes

- **Where:**
  - On Produkty, the Podroby card says "Sprzedaż prowadzi osobny dział w zakładzie uboju w Linne." Its button "Zapytaj o ofertę: Podroby wołowe" (`/zapytaj-o-oferte?produkt=podroby`) and the UPPZ section's button (`?produkt=uppz`) open the general offer form.
  - That form's side panel names "Dział sprzedaży MCKEEN-BEEF SO GmbH, Oelde". All offer enquiries go to one mailbox: `SALES_FORM_RECIPIENT` through `recipientFor('enquiry')` in `lib/mail`.
  - The home page sends the same need to `/kontakt?temat=offal-uppz#pomoc` (Monika Urbańczyk, Linne).
  - Sprzedaż i eksport does not mention the offal department at all.
- **Consequence:** offal and UPPZ requests reach the Oelde team instead of the department the page names. Buyers see two different answers to "whom do I ask".
- **Proposal:**
  - **Client decision first** (new question, with C-25): which mailbox handles offal and UPPZ enquiries?
  - **Smallest change:** the Podroby and UPPZ buttons lead to the Linne contact topic (as on the home page), and the form stays for the other products.
  - **Alternative:** the form routes by selected product to a second mailbox.
  - **Either way:** a short cross-link on Sprzedaż i eksport to the offal department.

### UXA-05 · P2 · Pages that end without a next step

- **Where:**
  - **Jakość** ends with "Środowisko" and only the link to the biogas project, with no route to the quality or audit contacts.
  - **Certyfikaty** ends with the archive and has no onward link at all.
  - **O firmie** ends with three text links, one of them the tax strategy (see UXA-14).
  - **Biogazownia** and **Strategia podatkowa** only have the breadcrumb.
- **Consequence:** visitors who finish reading have to go back to the menu. Auditors, the main readers of Jakość and Certyfikaty, are not led to the quality department.
- **Proposal:** short, context-specific closing blocks (UX-IA-001 decision 2, approved in direction), built from existing labels:
  - **Jakość:** Jakość / audyt (contact topic), Certyfikaty i archiwum, Produkty.
  - **Certyfikaty:** Jakość / audyt, System jakości, Produkty.
  - **O firmie:** Zakłady i spółki grupy, Kontakt, Zapytaj o ofertę.
  - **Biogazownia:** a back link to Środowisko on Jakość. Navigation only; the mandatory EU content stays untouched.

### UXA-06 · P2 · Desktop selector shows "Strona główna" on pages outside the menu

- **Where:** Zapytaj o ofertę, Biogazownia, Strategia podatkowa, Polityka prywatności, Wysłano and the 404 pages. The 3D selector's carriage rests on "Strona główna" (EN "Home", DE "Startseite"). `aria-current` is correctly empty and the breadcrumbs are correct.
- **Consequence:** sighted users are told they are on the home page.
- **Proposal:** no current tab on these pages (carriage hidden or neutral), or the parent section (Biogazownia → Jakość, Strategia podatkowa → O firmie). The navbar mechanics are frozen, so this needs the user's consent.

### UXA-07 · P2 · Certificate archive on phones

- **Where:** Certyfikaty, "Archiwum certyfikatów" at 390 px. The table is 460 px (PL; EN 443, DE 453) inside a 310 px scroll area. The "Pliki" header is cut to "Pli", and the PL/EN/DE buttons for the preview and the original PDF are off-screen. There is no visual cue that the area scrolls; it is keyboard-focusable and labelled.
- **Consequence:** on a phone, the files of expired certificates look unreachable.
- **Proposal:** below about 40rem, show each archive row as a stacked entry: year, standard, result and validity, then the file buttons. A smaller alternative is an edge shadow with a "przewiń" hint.

### UXA-08 · P2 · Certificate verification is not actionable

- **Where:** Certyfikaty, the note "Ważność certyfikatów można sprawdzić w bazach BRCGS Directory (brcdirectory.com) oraz IFS (ifs-certification.com)." Both names are plain text. The BRCGS Site Code and IFS COID needed to search those databases are not shown (GAP-03).
- **Consequence:** a QA employee who wants to verify the certificate has to search for the databases and still lacks the identifiers.
- **Proposal:** make both names external links (new tab, with the existing "opens in a new tab" pattern). Add the codes after the client confirms them (GAP-03).

### UXA-09 · P2 · Unbranded 404 for URLs with an extension

- **Where:** `/kontakt.html`, `/index.php`, `/assets/missing.png`, `/documents/certificates/missing.pdf`, `/photos/missing.webp`. They return Next.js's default English page ("404 · This page could not be found.") without header, footer or links. This happens although `global-not-found.tsx` (branded, Polish, with a link home) exists and `experimental.globalNotFound` is on.
- **Related edge case:** `/en/contact.html` renders the EN layout with the Polish heading "Nie znaleźliśmy tej strony".
- **Consequence:** outdated document links, for example a renamed certificate PDF or an old bookmark, end in a dead end.
- **Proposal:** find why the global not-found page is not used for these paths, and serve the branded page with links to the main sections.

### UXA-10 · P3 · Menu label "Eksport" against "Sprzedaż i eksport"

- **Where:** the desktop selector says "Eksport" (`navShort.sales`). The page H1, the mobile menu and the footer say "Sprzedaż i eksport"; EN "Export", DE "Export". It was chosen for space on 2026-10-06 (CHANGELOG, reconstructed).
- **Consequence:** a Polish domestic buyer may not look under "Eksport" for the sales contacts. The page covers "Polska – Rynek krajowy" as well.
- **Proposal:** consider "Sprzedaż", if it fits the selector; this needs the user's consent because the navbar is frozen. Otherwise keep it.

### UXA-11 · P3 · Header CTA on the enquiry page

- **Where:** "Zapytaj o ofertę" in the header on `/zapytaj-o-oferte`. The click opens no dialog, does not scroll and gives no feedback.
- **Proposal:** on that page, move focus to the first form field (or scroll to the form).

### UXA-12 · P3 · Heading of the markets ledger on Sprzedaż i eksport

### UXA-13 · P3 · Purchasing department on the plant cards

- **Where:** Zakłady, the SH and SH II cards. "Własny dział skupu żywca" is plain text, and the card's contact is the plant office.
- **Proposal:** link the bullet to `/kontakt?temat=sell-cattle#pomoc` (the purchasing director and department).

### UXA-14 · P3 · O firmie details

- **Where:**
  - The tax strategy link sits in "Po pierwsze klient", where it is out of context; the footer already has it.
  - The timeline reads 2012 → 2012–2015 → 2013, so the range entry interrupts the year order.
- **Proposal:**
  - Move the tax strategy link out of the customer paragraph, keeping it in the footer or adding a small "Dokumenty" line.
  - Place the 2012–2015 range after the single years it spans, or mark it as a period.

### UXA-15 · P3 · Contact page details

- **Where:**
  - The H1 "Kontakt" is the only subpage head without a lead.
  - The directory filter, for example "Zakład uboju, Linne", does not change the URL, so a filtered view cannot be shared or bookmarked.
- **Proposal:**
  - Use the router's existing intro sentence as the lead, or leave it as is.
  - Reflect the filter in the URL (as `?temat=` does for topics).

### UXA-16 · P3 · Small controls and small text

- **Where:**
  - The checkbox and radio boxes of the enquiry form are about 16–18 px. Their labels are clickable, so they remain usable.
  - The certified scope quote on certificate labels is 13 px, although the scope matters to auditors.
  - The breadcrumb link is 13 px.
- **Proposal:** controls of 20–24 px; the scope quote at 14–15 px.

### UXA-17 · P3 · Product cards show different rows

- **Where:**

  - Mięsa drobne has Zakład, Certyfikaty and Specyfikacja.
  - Ćwierci and Podroby have Forma and Zakład.

### UXA-18 · P3 · Company order differs between pages

## User tasks

1. **Importer looking for the offer and sales contact.**
   - **Path:** Home → product tile → Produkty `#elementy` → "Zapytaj o ofertę: Elementy wołowe" (dialog, product ticked): 2 clicks. Or the header or hero CTA: 1 click.
   - **Sales contacts:** the home sales panel, the footer on every page, the end of Produkty and Sprzedaż i eksport.
   - **Where it breaks:**
     - "Eksport" in the desktop menu (UXA-10).
     - On phones the sales phones are on screen 3 of Sprzedaż i eksport and at 77 % of Produkty (decision 3).
     - Offal buyers get contradictory routes (UXA-04).
2. **Quality employee who needs a plant's certificate.**
   - **Path:** menu → Certyfikaty → current BRCGS and IFS of the cutting plant with preview and original PDF in PL/EN/DE: 1–2 clicks. Quality contacts: Kontakt → "Jakość / audyt".
   - **Where it breaks:**
     - The slaughter plants are a dead end (UXA-03).
     - Verification is not actionable (UXA-08).
     - Expired certificates on a phone (UXA-07).
     - From Jakość the certificates are section 4 of 5 and there is no quality contact (decision 3, UXA-05).
3. **Cattle supplier looking for the right person.**
   - **Path:** Home, chain step 1 or the cooperation board "Sprzedaję bydło" → Kontakt with the topic open (Renata Zduniak, Linne; Małgorzata Będziechowska, Wydrzyn): 1 click. From the menu: Kontakt → "Sprzedaję bydło": 2 clicks.
   - **Where it breaks:** the Zakłady cards name the purchasing department without a link (UXA-13).
4. **Candidate.**
   - **Path:** menu → Kariera → job card → "Wyślij CV" (dialog) or call: 2 clicks. Validation guides the user.
   - **Where it breaks:**
     - EN/DE show the phone without +48 (GAP-13).
     - The recruitment privacy clause is missing (GAP-17, launch blocker).
5. **Foreign customer (EN/DE).**
   - **Path:** the language switch on every page keeps the page; all 14 routes exist with localised slugs; the footer says the translation is under review.
   - **Where it breaks:**
     - Old or mistyped EN/DE URLs trigger the request storm (UXA-01).
     - After sending a form, switching language reports a failure (UXA-02).
     - The contact topic is lost on switching (UXA-02).
     - EN/DE are not indexed yet (C-50).
     - Tax strategy PDFs exist in Polish only (labelled).
6. **Full data of one group company.**
   - **Path:** menu → Zakłady → structure card → company card → "Dane rejestrowe" (NIP, REGON, KRS, veterinary number, BDO): 2–3 clicks.
   - **Also:** the footer "Spółki grupy" (folded on phones) and the Kontakt directory.
   - **Where it breaks:** nothing blocking. The order differs between the three places (UXA-18, justified).
